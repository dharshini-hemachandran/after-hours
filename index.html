About

The questions that hold my attention sit between disciplines.

How does a ranking rule change who gets seen? Why does a résumé hide what a student can actually do? What makes people turn up to an event? Each one is technical, commercial and visual at once, and I would rather work in the overlap than pick a side.

So my work moves between rooms. In research I build simulations and test how fragile their conclusions are. In product I turn a problem into a matching system. In operations I write the plan other people can follow, and in creative direction I decide how it should feel to meet it. None of these is a hobby beside the others.
