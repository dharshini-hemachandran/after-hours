About

“If the path before you is clear, you’re following someone else’s.”

I’ve never been someone who could stick to just one way of thinking. Studying AI and ML got me interested in how things work, while research, entrepreneurship, and working with student communities showed me how much there is to learn beyond the technical side.

I’m drawn to understanding people, questioning the obvious, and figuring out how ideas become something real.

I like exploring things I’m unfamiliar with, even when I don’t have the entire picture figured out.

I’m still finding my way, but I’d rather follow what genuinely interests me, learn by doing, and see where it takes me than have everything planned out from the start.
