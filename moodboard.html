After Hours: moodboard

The primary reference is the animated Heaven Official's Blessing: its illustrated Ghost City streets, Paradise Manor and interiors. That artwork is the target for composition, colour and atmosphere but is not reproduced here or in the site. These photographs are supporting references for real timber architecture, lantern light and misty landscape. All are free to use under the Unsplash License and credited below.

What is taken from the references

  • Silhouette: stacked, curved eaves; tall tiered manor; dense street fronts that step back as they rise.
  • Light: a dark teal night with warm lantern pools; vermilion only where it is a lit accent.
  • Surface: painted, brush-marked texture rather than flat colour (and a painterly render pass over the 3D scene).
  • Depth: layered ridges and mist, each plane paler than the one before.
